Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications published by ALSAIDI on Google Play. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

1. Information Collection and Use

  • Local Storage: The app stores user preferences (reading progress, bookmarks, font size, and theme selection) locally on your device using a local SQLite/Room database. This data never leaves your device and is not collected or transmitted to external servers.
  • No Personal Data Collection: The app does not require registration, login, or personal information (such as name, email, address, or phone number) to read the novel.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and process information such as:

  • Advertising ID (GAID)
  • IP address and approximate device location
  • Device information and network connection status
  • Interaction with ads

This data is handled according to Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn more about how Google uses data here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

3. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

4. User Rights and Data Deletion

Since data is stored exclusively on your device, you can delete all saved data at any time by clearing the application's storage in your device Settings or uninstalling the app.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy, feel free to contact us at:
Email: technetworksapps@gmail.com
